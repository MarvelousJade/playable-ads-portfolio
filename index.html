Production ownership

Repeatable process, not one-off demos

The portfolio includes the tooling and quality gates needed to move from brief to reviewable creative to network artifact.

AI-assisted, deterministically validated

AI helps explore hooks, structured level candidates, test cases and implementation approaches. Creative JSON, semantic validation, human review and automated QA remain the source of truth; no AI service ships in the ad.

Read the documented workflow →

Config → validate → preview → export

Producer-editable variation is separated from gameplay logic. The build validates creative data, embeds code/config/art, reports budgets, and emits one HTML file. Playwright then drives complete funnels and rejects external dist requests.
